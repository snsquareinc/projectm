WHO WE ARE

Built on precision
and purpose

SN Square Group was founded on a simple belief: that the built environment shapes how people live, and that building well is an act of care for the communities we serve.

We are a Massachusetts-based development and general construction company with deep roots in residential and mixed-use construction. Every project we take on — from a single-family home to a multifamily development — is built with the same commitment to craft, transparency, and lasting quality.

We work closely with owners, developers, and communities across Massachusetts to deliver projects that stand the test of time.
